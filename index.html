Workflow

Stop switching tools for repeated actions.

Gesture Mesh keeps your modeling flow focused. Instead of moving between tools, menus, and operation panels, you work directly from the selected face, edge, or vertex.

01

Select geometry

Start from the face, edge, or vertex selection you already have in Blender Edit Mode.

02

Drag from selection

The drag direction chooses the operation, keeping the workflow direct and fast.

03

Release to apply

Preview the result while dragging, then release to commit the operation.

Face Mode

Direction-based face modeling.

In Face Select Mode, Gesture Mesh reads the direction of your drag and chooses the operation. Common modeling actions become fast, spatial, and direct.

Operations

Fast gestures for hard-surface details.

Extrude

Quick extrusion shapes

Drag up or down from selected faces to create extrusion shapes quickly.

Inset

Region and individual inset

Drag left for region inset or right for individual inset in Face Select Mode.

Bevel

Detail refinement

Drag diagonally to bevel selected faces, edges, or vertices without leaving the modeling flow.

Live Feedback

Preview before committing.

Gesture Mesh previews the operation while dragging. A small value label near the cursor shows the current amount for extrusion, inset, or bevel.

Release to confirm Esc or right click to cancel Shift for fine control
